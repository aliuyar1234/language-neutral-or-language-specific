\maketitle

The references listed below contained incorrect bibliographic metadata in the original version of
\emph{Language-Neutral or Language-Specific? Disentangling Multilingual LLM Subspaces with Naturalistic fMRI}.
The corrections do not affect any result or claim.

\section*{Li et al., 2022}
\textbf{As printed:} Jixing Li, Shohini Bhattasali, Shumin Zhang, Brent Franzluebbers, Wen-Ming Luh, R.\ Nathan Spreng, Jonathan R.\ Brennan, Yiming Yang, Christophe Pallier, and John Hale. Le petit prince multilingual naturalistic fmri corpus. \emph{Scientific Data}, 9(1):436, 2022. doi: 10.1038/s41597-022-01551-0.

\textbf{What was wrong:} two author given names, the article number, and the DOI.

\textbf{Corrected:} Jixing Li, Shohini Bhattasali, Shulin Zhang, Berta Franzluebbers, Wen-Ming Luh, R.\ Nathan Spreng, Jonathan R.\ Brennan, Yiming Yang, Christophe Pallier, and John Hale. Le Petit Prince multilingual naturalistic fMRI corpus. \emph{Scientific Data}, 9(1):530, 2022. doi: 10.1038/s41597-022-01625-7.

\section*{Nastase et al., 2021}
\textbf{As printed:} Samuel A.\ Nastase, Yibei Liu, Hannah Hillman, Kenneth A.\ Norman, and Uri Hasson. The ``narratives'' fmri dataset for evaluating models of naturalistic language comprehension. \emph{Scientific Data}, 8:250, 2021. doi: 10.1038/s41597-021-01033-3.

\textbf{What was wrong:} the author list. The second and third authors' names were misspelled, and the list was presented as complete although the paper has 25 authors, in which Kenneth A.\ Norman and Uri Hasson are the 24th and 25th.

\textbf{Corrected:} Samuel A.\ Nastase, Yun-Fei Liu, Hanna Hillman, Asieh Zadbood, Liat Hasenfratz, Neggin Keshavarzian, et al.\ (25 authors in total). The ``Narratives'' fMRI dataset for evaluating models of naturalistic language comprehension. \emph{Scientific Data}, 8:250, 2021. doi: 10.1038/s41597-021-01033-3.
